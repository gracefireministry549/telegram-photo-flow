# Business Agent System

A Telegram-controlled business automation system that discovers legitimate public business leads, researches problems, proposes solutions, and waits for the owner's approval before external outreach or other consequential actions.

## Approval-first workflow

1. Scout finds a relevant public lead.
2. Detective verifies the business and identifies a real, evidence-backed problem.
3. Qualifier scores the opportunity and rejects weak or unverifiable leads.
4. Solver prepares a practical service/solution and job plan.
5. Marketer prepares personalized outreach for review.
6. Owner approves, rejects, or edits the proposal.
7. Messenger handles approved client communication.
8. Job Manager tracks delivery.
9. Proof records what was actually completed.
10. Reporter sends important updates by email.

## Agents

- Scout — lead discovery
- Detective — evidence and problem research
- Qualifier — lead quality scoring
- Solver — solution and job planning
- Marketer — personalized outreach drafts
- Messenger — client conversation support
- Job Manager — job lifecycle tracking
- Proof — completion evidence
- Reporter — email notifications

## Safety and reliability rules

- Use public, relevant business information only.
- Never invent business facts, results, reviews, contacts, or credentials.
- Do not mass-spam or automatically contact leads without owner approval.
- Keep secrets in environment variables; never commit tokens or API keys.
- Mark a job completed only when there is evidence that the requested work was delivered.
- Reliability scores are based on recorded interactions and completed jobs, not guesses.
